Learning, check and delayed review

Practice offers feedback and optional hints. Checks hold all feedback until submission. The pilot criterion is at least 80% overall and 75% on text answers, on the first submission with fresh items.

Items seen in an earlier session, including abandoned sessions, are rehearsed. A retry remains useful but cannot become independent evidence. A finite bank may run out of fresh items.

Untested means no completed check / review first answer, not inability. Learning focus also uses completed learning sessions: recent misses or blanks first, then hint-supported answers, then untested items. Within that focus, new practice questions come first when available. Hints and rehearsal are learning, not independent evidence.

Each check samples 8 items and each review 4, with at least half text answers. Sections are spread where feasible; slot, text-answer and fresh-bank limits can leave sections out. Each session records its own sampling limits. Reading completion and earlier results are unchanged.
